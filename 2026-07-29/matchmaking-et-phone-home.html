Rebel's Puzzles

Rebel's Puzzles is a social experiment being conducted by Rebel's Dad on the patrons of Growlerz Trivia Night. He thinks this will be a fun addition to Caryn's awesome trivia questions. Like Rebel, Rebel's Dad responds well to feedback. Train him well.

Each Sunday, there will be a new puzzle on Rebel's Blog, followed by a new “Find It!” puzzle on Tuesday. These puzzles will provide hints to the weekly Growlerz Trivia Night puzzle.

Tonight's Puzzle

Tonight's puzzle is called “Matchmaking”. Match each clue on the left with exactly one answer from the word bank, writing one letter per blank. The circled letters will spell out a classic movie quote from a character whose real name is never given. Count letters only — ignore spaces and punctuation. Not all answers will fill the available letter blanks. One blank in each answer is circled — that’s the letter to circle. Read the circled letters top to bottom to reveal the Final Answer.

1 point per correct match · 5 points for the final answer.
