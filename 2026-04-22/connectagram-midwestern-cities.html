Tonight's Puzzle

Tonight's puzzle is called “Connectagram”. Fill in each answer using the clue. Some letters are circled — collect all the circled letters from every answer and unscramble them to reveal what all the answers have in common.

You get 1 point for each correct answer, and 5 points for finding the connection.

Example:
