Tonight's Puzzle

Tonight's puzzle is called “Matchmaking”. Match each item on the left with exactly one answer from the word bank. There is a single word that each answer has in common. Either the answer is a specific example of this word, or the answer paired with this word forms a specific example of this word. For example, if the word were DOG, both TERRIER and BULL would be acceptable answers. The number tells you which letter to circle in the matched answer. Read the circled letters top to bottom to reveal the Final Answer.

1 point per correct match · 5 points for the final answer.
