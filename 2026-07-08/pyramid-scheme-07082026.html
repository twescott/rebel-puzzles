Rebel’s Puzzles is a social experiment being conducted by Rebel’s Dad on the patrons of Growlerz Trivia Night. He thinks this will be a fun addition to Caryn’s awesome trivia questions. Like Rebel, Rebel’s Dad responds well to feedback. Train him well.

Tonight’s Puzzle

Tonight’s puzzle is called “Pyramid Scheme.” Kevin Bacon and the shaded squares are already filled in. Using the Answer Bank, complete the rest of the pyramid: each actor is joined by a line to the movies they starred in, and each movie to the actors who starred in it. Work your way down. 1 point per answer you place correctly (10 possible).

Example
This actor…
…starred in these movies…
…that also starred these actors…
