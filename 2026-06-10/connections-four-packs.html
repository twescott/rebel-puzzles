Tonight's Puzzle

Tonight's puzzle is called “Four Packs”. Sort the 16 words into four groups of four, where each group shares a hidden connection. Some words look like they belong with more than one group — but there is exactly one correct arrangement. You get 1 point per group you solve, for a maximum of 4 points.

Example — sort these 16 words…
DOGS
RATS
LIONS
BEER
MICE
EAGLES
CATS
DAY CARE
BEARS
ROACHES
HAMSTERS
GROOMING
DOLPHINS
ANTS
RABBITS
TRIVIA
…into these four groups:
Common pets:  DOGS · CATS · HAMSTERS · RABBITS
NFL teams:  LIONS · EAGLES · BEARS · DOLPHINS
Pests:  RATS · MICE · ROACHES · ANTS
Things you'll find at Growlerz:  DAY CARE · BEER · GROOMING · TRIVIA
Almost every word is an animal — the trick is sorting them into pets, pests, and team names, which leaves the odd group out.
