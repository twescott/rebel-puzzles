Rebel’s Puzzles

Rebel’s Puzzles is a social experiment being conducted by Rebel’s Dad on the patrons of Growlerz Trivia Night. He thinks this will be a fun addition to Caryn’s awesome trivia questions. Like Rebel, Rebel’s Dad responds well to feedback. Train him well.

Tonight’s Puzzle

Tonight’s puzzle is “Mini Jeopardy!” Four categories, four clues each. Each rounded square is a Jeopardy “answer” — you supply the “question.” Rows are worth 1, 2, 3, and 4 points top to bottom (harder as you go down). Pick ONE clue in each row — no two from the same category — and complete the response as a question in the blank at the bottom of that category’s column (each column is pre-led with its “Who is…?” / “What is…?”). Get it right: gain those points. Get it wrong: lose them. Then in Final Jeopardy, list every host you can: +1 for each correct, −1 for each wrong.
