Tonight’s Puzzle

Tonight’s puzzle is called “Pyramid Scheme.” Jane Austen and the shaded squares are already filled in. Using the Answer Bank, complete the rest of the pyramid: each line joins two boxes that belong together — an author to a book they wrote, a book to an actor from its screen adaptation, an actor to another film they were in, and that film to the novelist who wrote its source. Work your way down. 1 point per answer you place correctly (10 possible).
