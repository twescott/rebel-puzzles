Rebel’s Puzzles

Rebel’s Puzzles is a social experiment being conducted by Rebel’s Dad on the patrons of Growlerz Trivia Night. He thinks this will be a fun addition to Caryn’s awesome trivia questions. Like Rebel, Rebel’s Dad responds well to feedback. Train him well.

Tonight’s Puzzle

Tonight’s puzzle is called “Triple Play.” Every row is one podcast, split across three columns: Podcast, Host, Previous Role. To play, each row is one podcast. One box in every row is already filled in — work out the other two: the show, the celebrity who hosts it, and the role that made them famous first. There is an Answer Bank for each column, and it holds more words than you need. 1 point per box you fill in correctly (12 possible).
