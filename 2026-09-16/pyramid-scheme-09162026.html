Tonight’s Puzzle

Tonight’s puzzle is called “Pyramid Scheme.” Mexico and the shaded squares are already filled in. Using the Answer Bank, complete the rest of the pyramid: each line joins two places that share a border. Work your way down. 1 point per answer you place correctly (11 possible).
