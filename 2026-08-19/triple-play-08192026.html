Tonight’s Puzzle

Tonight’s puzzle is called “Triple Play.” Every row is one team, split across three columns: City, Team, Highest scorer in team history. To play, each row is one team. One box in every row is already filled in — work out the other two. Teams are listed by nickname only, so you have to know which city each one plays in. Some answers may be used in more than one row. There is an Answer Bank for each column, and it holds more words than you need. 1 point per box you fill in correctly (10 possible).
