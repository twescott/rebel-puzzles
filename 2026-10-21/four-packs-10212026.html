Rebel's Puzzles

Rebel's Puzzles is a social experiment being conducted by Rebel's Dad on the patrons of Growlerz Trivia Night. He thinks this will be a fun addition to Caryn's awesome trivia questions. Like Rebel, Rebel's Dad responds well to feedback. Train him well.

Each Sunday, there will be a new puzzle on Rebel's Blog, followed by a new “Find It!” puzzle on Tuesday. These puzzles will provide hints to the weekly Growlerz Trivia Night puzzle.

Tonight's Puzzle

Tonight's puzzle is called “Four Packs”. Sort the 16 words into four groups of four, where each group shares a hidden connection. Some words look like they belong with more than one group — but there is exactly one correct arrangement. Write your four groups in the answer boxes, then score yourself: 1 point for each group you complete (all four words correct) and 1 point for each correct connection — for a maximum of 8 points.
